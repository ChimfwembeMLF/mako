# How to manage content in Mako

This guide covers the everyday path from idea → draft → published post.

## Quick path (most days)

1. Open **Content Engine**
2. Write or generate a post
3. Attach an image if needed
4. Choose the platforms
5. Save as draft, or schedule / publish
6. Check **Scheduler** and **Social** afterward

---

## Create a post

### Open Content Engine

From the Create menu, open **Content Engine**.

### Option A — Write it yourself

1. Start a new post.
2. Write your copy.
3. Pick the platforms (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and so on).
4. Adjust the wording for each platform if you want different versions.
5. Attach media from your library, or upload a new file.
6. Save.

### Option B — Let AI help

1. Start a new post and choose generate / AI options.
2. Give a theme, goal, or rough draft (for example: “launch discount for spring”).
3. Review the suggested copy.
4. Edit anything that doesn’t sound like your brand.
5. Adapt for each platform if offered.
6. Attach or generate images if you want visuals.
7. Save.

**Tip:** If AI output feels off-brand, update **Brand Brain** first, then try again.

### Edit an existing post

Open the post from Content Engine (or open it by its detail page) and make changes while it’s still a draft. After it’s published, treat changes carefully — some platforms can’t be edited the same way once live.

---

## Use the media library

Open **Media** from the Library menu.

1. Upload images or other assets you reuse often.
2. When creating a post, attach files from the library instead of re-uploading.

You can also generate images inside Content Engine for a specific post.

---

## Campaigns and templates (optional)

### Campaigns

Open **Campaigns** when you want a series of related posts (for example a product launch week). Plan several posts together instead of one-offs.

### Post templates

Open **Post Templates** to save copy you reuse (promotions, weekly tips, event reminders). Start from a template in Content Engine to save time.

---

## Schedule a post

1. Open **Scheduler**.
2. Find your draft in the queue or calendar.
3. Choose the date and time.
4. Confirm the platforms.
5. Save the schedule.

Your post will publish automatically at that time if your account is set up for scheduled publishing.

You can also schedule from Content Engine when you finish writing.

---

## Publish now

1. Open the post in **Content Engine** or **Scheduler**.
2. Use **Publish** (or the publish panel).
3. Confirm the connected accounts.
4. Publish.

Check the result afterward under **Social**. If a channel fails, the post may still succeed on other channels — fix the connection and try that channel again if needed.

---

## If your team uses approvals

Some teams require a review before anything goes live.

1. Creators save a **draft** and submit it for approval.
2. Reviewers open **Approvals**.
3. They approve or send it back with feedback.
4. After approval, a publisher schedules or publishes.

See [Team roles & approvals](./team-roles-and-approvals.md) for who can do what.

---

## Checklist before you hit publish

- [ ] Copy matches your brand voice
- [ ] Correct platforms selected
- [ ] Image or video attached where required (especially Instagram)
- [ ] Links and hashtags checked
- [ ] Date and time correct (if scheduling)
- [ ] Right accounts connected under **Connections**
