# Getting started with Mako

Follow these steps the first time you use Mako. You only need to do most of this once.

## 1. Sign in

1. Open Mako in your browser.
2. Go to **Sign in**.
3. Create an account or sign in with email, Google, or LinkedIn.

Your first sign-in sets up your company space and a default workspace for you. You are the owner of that space.

## 2. Complete the welcome wizard

If this is your first visit, a short welcome flow may appear.

1. Enter your **company name**.
2. Add a short **description** of what you do.
3. Continue.

You can refine everything later in Brand Brain.

## 3. Set up Brand Brain

Open **Brand Brain** from the Create menu.

Fill in as much as you can. Better brand details mean better AI suggestions later.

Include:

- Company name and what you offer
- Who your audience is
- Tone of voice (for example: friendly, expert, playful)
- Offers, promotions, or key messages
- Words or topics to avoid
- Competitors and keywords (optional)
- Your website (optional — Mako can learn from it)
- Brand documents (optional)

Save when you’re done.

**Tip:** Spend 10–15 minutes here before generating posts. It pays off.

## 4. Connect your social accounts

Open **Connections**.

Connect the channels you want to publish to, such as:

- Facebook Page
- Instagram
- LinkedIn
- X (Twitter)
- YouTube
- TikTok
- WhatsApp

You can create drafts without connecting accounts, but you need connections before posts can go live.

## 5. (Optional) Invite your team

If other people will write or approve content:

1. Open **Team**.
2. Invite people by email.
3. Give each person a role that matches their job (see [Team roles & approvals](./team-roles-and-approvals.md)).

## 6. (Optional) Add another workspace

Use **Workspaces** if you manage more than one brand or business unit. Each workspace can have its own Brand Brain and content.
