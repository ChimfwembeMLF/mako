# After you publish

What to do once posts are live.

## Social home

Open **Social** for a quick view of your publishing activity and what’s going on across channels.

## Reply to comments and messages

Open **Social Inbox** (Replies) to:

- See comments and messages from connected channels
- Reply from Mako where supported
- Handle WhatsApp chats if WhatsApp is connected

Keeping the inbox clear helps you respond quickly and keeps your brand consistent.

## Check performance

- **Analytics** — how content and channels are doing
- **Reports** — pull insights when you need a summary for a meeting or client

Use these to decide what to create next week.

## WhatsApp and chatbot (if you use them)

These sit next to content work but are separate tools:

- **WhatsApp** — messaging, menus, and templates
- **Chatbot** / **Knowledge** — answers for your website or embeddable assistant, based on documents you upload

They don’t replace Content Engine for social posts.

## If something didn’t publish

1. Open the post and check which platforms succeeded or failed.
2. Go to **Connections** and confirm the account is still linked.
3. Fix the connection if needed, then try publishing that channel again.
4. If you’re unsure, ask your workspace Owner or Admin for help.

## Everyday routine (suggested)

| When | Do this |
|------|---------|
| Morning | Check **Scheduler** for today’s posts |
| During the day | Create drafts in **Content Engine**; clear **Social Inbox** |
| End of week | Skim **Analytics** / **Reports**; update **Brand Brain** if offers changed |
