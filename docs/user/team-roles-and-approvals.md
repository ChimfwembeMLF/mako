# Team roles and approvals

Use this if more than one person works in Mako.

## Roles (simple view)

| Role | What they usually do |
|------|----------------------|
| **Owner** | Full control: brand, billing, team, publishing |
| **Admin** | Manage brand, team, and most settings; can approve and publish |
| **Publisher** | Approve, schedule, and publish posts |
| **Creator** | Write and edit drafts; may need approval before publish |
| **Viewer** | Look at content and reports without changing them |

Exact permissions can vary if an admin customized roles. When in doubt, ask your Owner or Admin.

## Invite someone

1. Open **Team**.
2. Send an invite to their email.
3. Choose the role that matches their job.
4. They accept the invite and sign in.

## Approvals (maker-checker)

If your company wants a second pair of eyes before posts go live:

1. An Admin turns on approval rules under **Maker-Checker** (in admin/settings areas).
2. Creators prepare drafts and submit them for review.
3. Publishers or Admins open **Approvals**.
4. They **approve** (ready to schedule/publish) or **reject** (send back for edits).

Without approvals enabled, people with publish access can schedule or publish drafts directly.

## Good team habits

- Keep Brand Brain up to date so everyone’s AI drafts stay consistent.
- Use Campaigns for launches so the whole series is visible in one place.
- Review scheduled posts in **Scheduler** every morning.
- Don’t share login passwords — invite each person with their own account.
